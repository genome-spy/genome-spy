<!-- category: feature -->

Add a `raster` mark for dense heatmaps, sequence alignments, and decoded images.

The HiGlass data source loads contact maps at multiple resolutions in
rectangular views and single-axis triangle tracks. Data rasters show cell
tooltips by default and support brush-controlled opacity. Packed-color images
can opt in to tooltips. SVG export embeds raster layers as images and keeps
surrounding vector layers editable.
