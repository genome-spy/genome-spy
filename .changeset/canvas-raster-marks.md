<!-- category: feature -->

Add a `raster` mark for dense heatmaps, sequence alignments, and decoded images.

The HiGlass data source loads contact maps at multiple resolutions in
rectangular views and single-axis triangle tracks. Data rasters support
brush-controlled opacity and show cell values and positions on index and locus
scales in tooltips. Packed-color images can opt in to tooltips. SVG export
embeds raster layers as images and keeps surrounding vector layers editable.
