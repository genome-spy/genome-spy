<!-- category: feature -->

Add a `raster` mark for dense heatmaps, sequence alignments, and decoded images.

The HiGlass source loads contact maps as you pan and zoom, and triangle
projection aligns maps and annotations on a single genomic axis. Raster cells
support tooltips and selection conditions. A cell field can drive color or
opacity. SVG export embeds raster layers as images and preserves surrounding
vector marks.
