Light and dark mode

Change the appearance of one running visualization. System follows your browser’s preference; Light and Dark override it. The host reads six CSS colors and updates GenomeSpy’s palette parameter.

Scroll over the plot to zoom and Shift-drag to brush a region, then switch appearance. Zoom and selection stay in place. Data colors and the red annotation stay the same; titles, guides, legends, neutral lines, and backgrounds follow the palette. Download PNG or SVG with the plot’s controls.
